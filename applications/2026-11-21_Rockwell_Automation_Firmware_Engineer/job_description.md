Job Description

**Position Summary**

As part of our development engineering expansion footprint, we are hiring Firmware Security Engineers from the fresh graduate pool to join our global Power Motion Business (PMB) development organization in the Singapore Campus.

This is an entry-level role for graduates with a strong foundation in embedded systems, C/C++ programming and security fundamentals, and a keen interest in embedded firmware security and real-time control systems. You will work under the guidance of experienced engineers. You will contribute to the design, development, and testing of secure embedded firmware subsystems. This work will involve learning how to balance run-time performance, memory requirements, simplicity, and correctness. Additionally, you will learn about reusability, reliability, security, and safety. You will learn to apply secure-by-design principles and to reuse existing internal or external firmware components, security libraries, and frameworks to shorten the product development cycle. We consider cybersecurity training or certification a plus, and we will provide structured on-the-job mentoring.

You will work with stakeholders within the region and globally. You will report into the Singapore Engineering Manager in Singapore, with functional support and mentorship through the global development engineering team.

**Your Responsibilities:**

- Help analyze, designing, programming, debugging, and modifying firmware, and troubleshoot code for firmware applications, with guidance from senior engineers. Gain exposure to analog and digital hardware and firmware operating systems.
- Help develop cybersecurity solutions across embedded and industrial automation product platforms, and learn to apply secure-by-design principles throughout the product development lifecycle.
- Support the design, development, and testing of embedded security technologies for drives products, including:
- Secure Boot
- Secure Firmware Update
- Cryptographic Key and Certification Management
- Device Identity and Authentication
- Secure Communications
- Vulnerability Management
- Product Hardening
- Security Monitoring and Logging
- Participate in cybersecurity architecture reviews, threat modeling activities, security risk assessments, and mitigation planning, with guidance from senior engineers.
- Assist in vulnerability remediation and product security improvements. Additionally, helps investigate firmware security anomalies and field-reported cybersecurity issues together with the product security team, functional test team, customer support team, or other groups.
- Support compliance with cybersecurity standards and regulatory requirements such as IEC 62443, NIST, and ISA/IEC industrial security requirements, and with internal secure development lifecycle processes.
- Learns and applies secure coding practices, cryptography, embedded security, and industrial cybersecurity best practices, using C/C++, Python and scripting languages.
- Supports security verification by maintaining the link between security requirements and testing, and by writing unit, integration and security tests.
- Follows and helps improve engineering processes and best practices, including code reviews, coding standards, and documentation.
- Keeps learning about embedded firmware engineering and cybersecurity methodologies and technologies relevant to our industry through training, self-study, and seminars.
- Willingness to work in Agile and Scrum firmware development frameworks as the business demands.
- Builds working relationships with colleagues, mentors, and key contacts across firmware, product security, systems, hardware, and quality organizations.
- Works effectively in a globally distributed team environment, growing from supporting tasks to owning well-defined modules.

**The Essentials – You Will Have**

- We require a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related discipline. The degree should include coursework or projects in embedded systems, firmware, or cybersecurity. Fresh graduates or those graduating within the year are welcome to apply.
- Master's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering or Cybersecurity is an advantage but not required.
- No prior full-time experience required. Internships, final-year projects, or personal projects involving embedded C/C++ and embedded security are highly valued.
- Good foundation in C/C++ programming and data structures; working knowledge of Python or scripting languages is desired.
- Basic understanding of security fundamentals such as the CIA triad, threat modeling (e.g., STRIDE), cryptography, PKI, certificates, and secure boot is desired. Familiarity with hardware security modules (HSM) or secure elements is a plus.
- Basic understanding of communication interfaces (e.g., UART, SPI, I2C) is desired. Familiarity with security protocols such as TLS, or DTLS is a plus.
- Familiarity with Git or similar version control tools.
- Awareness of agile processes such as Scrum is desired.
- Problem-solving and debugging skills, with attention to writing clean, maintainable and secure code.
- Learn, accept feedback, and take on new challenges, with the ability to work both independently and as part of a team in a matrixed organization.
- Good verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to explain technical information clearly and concisely to a globally distributed team.
