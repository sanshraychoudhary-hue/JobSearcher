## Job Description

Join our dynamic team as a Senior Firmware Engineer and be at the forefront of embedded software innovation! We're seeking a talented professional to create cutting-edge firmware solutions that power tomorrow's electronic devices.

**What will you do:**

- Design and develop sophisticated firmware applications, creating robust embedded software solutions for electronic devices
- Research and implement advanced firmware algorithms while working within hardware constraints and system complexity
- Execute comprehensive testing, debugging, and troubleshooting throughout the development lifecycle
- Collaborate with programmers and hardware design engineers to optimize firmware performance
- Create detailed technical documentation and specifications for firmware operations

**What will make you successful:**

- Proven expertise in embedded software development and 2D/3D graphics engines
- Strong proficiency in machine language, assembly language, and programming languages like C/C++
- Demonstrated ability to develop and execute test plans ensuring specification compliance
- Experience with firmware algorithm design and implementation
- Strong problem-solving skills with attention to technical details

**What's in it for you:**

- Opportunity to work on cutting-edge embedded systems and innovative electronic devices
- Autonomy to propose and develop solutions in a technical domain
- Professional growth through exposure to complex technical challenges
- Collaborative environment working with cross-functional engineering teams
- Platform to establish yourself as a technical thought leader in firmware development
