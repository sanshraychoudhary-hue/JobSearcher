## Original Posting Content

Griffinmark Robotics Pvt. Ltd. is looking for a motivated and enthusiastic *Embedded Engineer – Fresher* to join our Engineering and R&D team. The candidate will work on embedded systems development, microcontroller programming, hardware interfacing, and firmware development for robotics, automation, and intelligent electromechanical products.

The ideal candidate should have a strong foundation in electronics, basic programming skills, problem-solving abilities, and a willingness to learn and work on real-world engineering projects.

*Key Responsibilities

*

* Assist in developing, testing, and debugging embedded systems and firmware.
* Write and maintain embedded C/C++ code for microcontrollers.
* Work with microcontrollers such as ESP32, STM32, Arduino, or similar platforms.
* Interface sensors, actuators, motors, displays, and communication modules with embedded controllers.
* Assist in hardware testing, circuit debugging, and prototype development.
* Work with communication protocols such as UART, SPI, I2C, CAN, and GPIO.
* Support PCB testing, hardware integration, and firmware-hardware interfacing.
* Use basic electronic test equipment such as a multimeter, oscilloscope, and logic analyser.
* Identify and troubleshoot hardware and software issues.
* Collaborate with mechanical, electronics, robotics, and software engineering teams.
* Prepare technical documentation, test reports, and development notes.
* Follow engineering standards, product safety requirements, and quality procedures.

*Required Skills And Qualifications

*

* B.E./B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE), Electronics Engineering, Instrumentation, or a related discipline.
* Basic knowledge of C/C++ programming.
* Understanding of microcontrollers, embedded systems, and digital electronics.
* Basic knowledge of sensors, actuators, electronic circuits, and interfacing.
* Familiarity with any microcontroller development platform, such as Arduino, ESP32, or STM32, is an advantage.
* Basic understanding of communication protocols such as UART, SPI, and I2C.
* Good analytical, troubleshooting, and problem-solving skills.
* Willingness to learn new technologies and work in a hands-on engineering environment.
* Good communication and teamwork skills.

*Preferred Qualifications

*

* Academic projects involving microcontrollers, robotics, IoT, or automation.
* Familiarity with PCB design tools or circuit simulation software.
* Basic knowledge of Git, debugging tools, and firmware development environments.
* Exposure to motor control, sensor integration, or real-time embedded systems.
* Participation in technical projects, workshops, internships, or engineering competitions.

*What We Offer

*

* Opportunity to work on real-world robotics and automation products.
* Hands-on experience in embedded hardware and firmware development.
* Mentorship and technical guidance from the engineering team.
* Exposure to product prototyping, testing, debugging, and integration.
* Opportunities for professional growth and skill development.

*Application Process

* Interested candidates may submit their updated resume along with details of relevant academic projects, internships, or personal technical projects.
